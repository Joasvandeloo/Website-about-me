About Kings.

"Kings" is a story written by me (check the About Me page). When I was younger, I was always imagining people with superpowers — I called them Kings. Later I tried a few times to create a story about it, until I bought a drawing book and started the script for a manga or something. At the moment I'm learning how to draw; if I finish the drawing book, I'm going to make it. I already have the story for a chapter and am writing the second one. Maybe in a month or so I can start writing. And if it gets enough hype, I might make some merch, because it would be so cool to bring a story to a physical object, like a mug or a shirt with a cool design. So now, if you want, you can check out the other pages about the story — I recommend checking out the world first.

Combat ranks

  • Novice – Beginner
  • Apprentice – Learning the fundamentals
  • Adept – Competent and experienced
  • Expert – Highly skilled
  • Master – Exceptional mastery
  • Grandmaster – Master-level skill with extensive experience
  • Legendary Master – Extremely rare, almost mythical skill
  • Transcendent – Beyond normal human ability

Characters:

Fire

"Fire" is not his real name — his real name is unknown to everyone. He is an apprentice of Death. Lia, Jacki, and Brine are his friends. He uses a katana as his weapon.

Combat Rank: At least legendary master

Lia

She is friends with Fire and Jacki. She is the Queen of Water; her weapon is a spear. She doesn't like it when Jacki goes to the bar — technically he is very old because he is immortal, but he keeps the body of a 15-year-old. She doesn't like the idea of someone with a young body drinking.

Combat Rank: Adept

Jacki

Jacki is getting trained by Fire, he like's going to the bar with Fire what Lia doesn't like about him. Sometimes he and Fire go to a fighting club to have fun and win some money. He is also Friends with Lia just doesn't like the same things as her.

Combat Rank: Expert

Brine

He is the King of Lightning; he too is an apprentice of Death, and he is good friends with Fire — together, nobody can take them. He is also a reality hacker, which means he can change anything within a dimension. He is the only one with this power, and also the guardian of it, since others could literally destroy whole dimensions with it.

Combat Rank: About the same level as Fire

Death

He is the King of Death; his real name is unknown — people call him Death, and maybe that is just his name. He is very dangerous, and the master of Fire and Brine. Don't mess with this guy. He is the only one with the highest combat level; only his apprentices come close to his level.

Combat Rank: Transcendent

King of dark lords

He wants to be called King of Dark Lords, to the point where he calls himself that and never mentions that he had a different name — the name already says who he is. Dark Lords and Ladies weren't just created when reality came into being. If you have an extreme amount of hate, you become one; you can use it, but you'll become more powerful if you control your anger so you can channel it into attacks. They all have the same powers — only the King has the most hate inside him. Also, they're not all just evil.

Combat Rank: Combat master, maybe higher
